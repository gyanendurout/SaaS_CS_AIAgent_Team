# SECTION 1: RETURNS, EXCHANGES & REFUNDS

## 1.1 General Return & Exchange Policy

- **Who can initiate:** Only the **original purchaser** can initiate a return or exchange.
- **Time window:** Customers have **30 calendar days from the date of purchase** to return or exchange an item — but only for items purchased **directly from JOOLA**.
- **Authorization required:** All returns and exchanges must first be authorized. The customer must contact JOOLA Customer Service to receive a **Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number** before sending anything back.
- **Proof of purchase required:** A receipt or proof of purchase must accompany every return.

---

## 1.2 Item Condition Requirements for Returns/Exchanges

Items must be:
- **New and unused**
- **In original packaging** with all accessories included

### Product-Specific Condition Rules:
| Product | Condition Required |
|---|---|
| Paddles | Seal over the grip handle must be **intact and unbroken** |
| Apparel | Original tags must still be **attached**; cannot be worn or washed |
| Table Tennis Rubber(s), Nets, Balls | Cannot be returned if **opened**, unless the item is **defective** |

---

## 1.3 Shipping Charges for Returns

- **Standard returns:** Customer is responsible for additional shipping and handling charges.
- **JOOLA's error or defect:** If the return/exchange is a result of JOOLA's error or a product defect, the customer will **NOT** be charged for return shipping and handling.

---

## 1.4 Refund Policy

- Refunds can only be issued to the **original purchaser**.
- Refunds are issued to the **original method of payment** only.
- Once the returned item is received and inspected, the customer will be **notified of the approval or rejection**.
- If approved, the refund will be **processed within 7–10 business days**.

---

## 1.5 Order Cancellation Policy

- **Orders cannot be cancelled or edited once placed.** This is a hard policy with no exceptions.
- If the customer no longer wants the item after receiving it, they must contact customer support to **initiate a return**.
- The item must be in **brand-new condition** and returned in its **original packaging**.

> **Voice Agent Script for Cancellation Requests:**
> *"I understand you'd like to cancel your order. Unfortunately, once an order is placed, we're unable to cancel or make any changes to it. However, once you receive the item, as long as it's in brand-new, unopened condition, you can contact us to start a return. Would you like me to help you with that process once your order arrives?"*

---

## 1.6 Items Purchased Through Authorized Retailers (Third-Party)

- Customers who purchased from an **authorized retailer** (e.g., a sports store, Amazon authorized seller) should contact their **original point of purchase within 30 days** for returns or exchanges.
- They should only contact JOOLA directly to file a **warranty claim** — not a return or exchange — if the retailer cannot resolve the issue.

> **Voice Agent Note:** Do not process return/exchange requests for items bought at third-party retailers. Direct customers back to their purchase location first.

---

## 1.7 Items Purchased Through Unauthorized Retailers

- Items purchased from **unauthorized retailers** (e.g., eBay, Facebook Marketplace, auction sites) are **NOT covered** by JOOLA's return, exchange, or warranty policies.
- JOOLA cannot verify the authenticity, condition, or purchase history of such items.

> **Voice Agent Script:**
> *"I'm sorry to hear about the issue. Unfortunately, items purchased from unauthorized marketplaces like eBay or Facebook Marketplace are not eligible for returns or warranty coverage through JOOLA. For future purchases, we recommend buying directly from joola.com or one of our authorized retailers to ensure full coverage."*

---

## 1.8 Final Sale Items

### What is Final Sale?
- Items labeled as **"Final Sale"** are NOT eligible for return, refund, or exchange.
- All **clearance items** and items sold at a discounted price (shown in **red** on the website) are considered final sale.
- **Black Friday and Holiday 2024 discounts** (except the Apparel 30% off promotion) were all final sale.

### Exceptions for Final Sale Items:
Final sale items **may** be eligible for resolution only if:
- The item is **defective**
- The item was **damaged** upon arrival
- The item was **incorrectly shipped**
- The issue must be **reported within 7 days of receipt**

### No Price Adjustments:
- Clearance/final sale items are **not eligible for price adjustments or price matches** under any circumstances. Pricing is fixed after purchase.

> **Voice Agent Script for Final Sale:**
> *"Since this item was purchased as a final sale/clearance item, it's generally not eligible for returns or exchanges. However, if you received an item that's defective, damaged, or not what you ordered, and you're reporting this within 7 days of receiving it, we may be able to help. Can you describe the issue you're experiencing?"*

## 2.8 Warranty Claim Process (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1 — Submission
- Contact JOOLA Customer Service through the **case portal** on the Contact Us page.
- Hours of operation: **Monday–Friday, 8am–5pm EST** (closed on major holidays)
- Required information:
  - Full name
  - Mailing address
  - Daytime telephone number
  - SKU number of the product
  - Description of the problem

### Step 2 — Verification
- JOOLA will send a message requesting:
  - **Copy of original proof of purchase**
  - **Photo or video documentation** of the damage or defect
- JOOLA will verify the item was purchased from JOOLA or an authorized retailer.
- Only the **original purchaser** can file a warranty claim.

### Step 3 — Review & Next Steps
- After review, JOOLA will provide instructions to either:
  - **Ship the product back** to JOOLA, OR
  - Complete a **void and break process** (product is destroyed by customer per JOOLA's written instructions)
- This is determined **case-by-case**; written instructions will always be provided first.

### Step 4 — Repair or Replacement
- JOOLA will repair or replace the defective product **at their sole discretion**.
- Replacements will be the **same model** as the original.
- If the original model is **discontinued**, JOOLA may offer a comparable model at its discretion.
- **Upgrades or changes to a different model are not permitted.**
- Replacement products inherit the **remaining warranty period** from the original product's purchase date (not a new warranty period).
- Replacement will only be issued after the original item is:
  - Returned to JOOLA, OR
  - Void and break process completed

### Step 5 — Replacement Limits
| Warranty Type | Maximum Replacements |
|---|---|
| Standard 6-month paddle warranty | **2 replacements** |
| Extended 12-month NFC registered warranty | **3 replacements** |

> **Voice Agent Note on Replacement Limits:** If a customer has already received the maximum number of replacements, they are no longer eligible for warranty replacements. Communicate this clearly and empathetically.

# SECTION 3: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ RESPONSES)

## 3.1 How do I register a pickleball paddle with an NFC chip?

**Answer:** Download the JOOLA Infinity app on iOS or Android. Open the app, go to **More > Product Settings > Product Registration** and follow the prompts. You can also tap your paddle with the app open to initiate registration. To qualify for the extended 12-month warranty, registration must be completed within **14 days of purchase** with a valid receipt uploaded.

---

## 3.2 How do I register a paddle WITHOUT an NFC chip?

**Answer:** You can still register your paddle using the JOOLA Infinity app (same navigation: More > Product Settings > Product Registration). Alternatively, you can register manually online without the app by entering your information and uploading your receipt on the JOOLA website.

---

## 3.3 How do I cancel my Infinity App subscription?

**Answer:** Visit **www.infinity.joola.com/home** from a **web browser** (not the mobile app) and follow the cancellation steps. If you've missed the 30-day free trial window and need help with a future charge, contact JOOLA Customer Support before your next billing date — previous charges cannot be refunded.

---

## 3.4 Where does JOOLA ship to?

**Answer:** JOOLA ships to all **50 US states**, Washington D.C., and Puerto Rico.

---

## 3.5 Is two-day shipping guaranteed?

**Answer:** No. Two-day shipping is a **service**, not a guaranteed delivery date. Please allow **1–2 business days for order processing** before shipping begins. Delays may occur due to carrier issues, weather, or other unexpected events. Two-day shipping is also **not available** for:
- Table Tennis Tables
- Pro Nets
- Large or wholesale orders
- Orders with special shipping requirements (LTL freight)

---

## 3.6 Can I use a discount or promo code on a new product?

**Answer:** Newly launched products are **not eligible for any promotions, discounts, or upgrades for the first 90 days** after launch. After 90 days, applicable promotions may be used.

---

## 3.7 What is a "Final Sale" item?

**Answer:** Final sale items — including all clearance products and items shown at a discounted/red price on the website — cannot be returned, refunded, or exchanged. The only exceptions are items that are defective, damaged, or incorrectly shipped, reported **within 7 days of receipt**.

---

## 3.8 What is the Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) process?

**Answer:** Before sending any item back, the customer must contact JOOLA Customer Service to open a case. Once the purchase and request are verified, JOOLA will issue an **RMA number**. Customers must include this number with their return. Returns without an RMA number may affect eligibility for a refund or warranty support. In-person returns are not accepted.

---

## 3.9 My order arrived damaged — what do I do?

**Answer:**
- For **tables/freight items**: If the damage is visible at delivery, **refuse the shipment**. Contact JOOLA immediately.
- For **all other items**: Contact JOOLA Customer Service within the appropriate window (7 days for final sale items; 30 days for standard items), provide photo or video evidence, and open a customer service case.

---

## 3.10 I want to return apparel — what are the rules?

**Answer:** Apparel can be returned within 30 days of purchase if:
- Original tags are still attached
- It has **not** been worn or washed
- It is in brand-new condition with original packaging

---

## 3.11 What does UPA-A Certified mean?

**Answer:** The UPA-A (United Pickleball Association of America) is the governing body for the United Pickleball Association (UPA), which oversees the PPA Tour and Major League Pickleball. UPA-A Certified products meet the standards set by this governing body.

---

## 3.12 How do I find product instructions or assembly guides?

**Answer:** JOOLA has a dedicated resource page with product specifications, assembly videos, and full instruction manuals. Direct the customer to the **Product Instructions** section on joola.com.

---

## 3.13 How do I find the right apparel or shoe size?

**Answer:** JOOLA's apparel size chart uses measurements in **inches** based on body measurements. If chest and waist measurements suggest different sizes, order based on **chest or waist measurement** (whichever is preferred). All shoe sizes are listed in **men's sizes**.
