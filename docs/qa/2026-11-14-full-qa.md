## Defects found and fixed

| ID | Severity | Symptom | Root cause | Fix | Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BUG-1 | **Critical** (pre-existing) | `lookup_order {"order_number":"%"}` returned another customer's name, email, phone; `"X,order_number.neq.x"` faked a registration / eligibility | order number used unescaped in `.ilike()` and pasted into `.or()` filter strings; `/api/tools/*` reachable from the internet | strict `OrderNumberField` (letters, digits, space, `#`, `-`, ≤40), `escapeLike()` on every email/name ILIKE (tools + customer routes), and `/api/tools/*` now only accepts in-process calls from the signed Vapi webhook (per-boot token, `lib/internal-tools.ts`) → direct calls 403 | attack script: 15/15; signed webhook still answers every tool |
| BUG-2 | Medium | "hyperion cas 13.5" flagged as closest match | `13.5` split into `13` + `5` | keep decimals in numbers as one token | `test_decimal_thickness_stays_one_keyword` |
| BUG-3 | Medium | every product `url: null` | sandbox products not on Online Store channel | fall back to `https://<store>/products/<handle>` | `test_url_falls_back_to_product_handle…` |
| BUG-4 | Medium | 10 000-char order number → 500 after 10 s | no max length on tool inputs | max lengths on all tool fields | 400 in 0.18 s |
| BUG-5 | Medium | `purchase_date: 2026-13-45` → 503 leaking internal URL | regex-only date check | real calendar-date validation | 400, no leak (also Feb 30) |
| BUG-6 | Medium | first burst after idle: 4–9/20 live-tool calls `enabled:false` | cold Vercel Python start ~5.9 s > 5 s API wait | live-tool timeout 8 s (Vapi limit 20 s) | warm bursts 0/20 errors |
| BUG-7 | Medium | 5 wrong postcodes locked a real order for 15 min (lock-out by strangers) | every miss counted, brake blocked email too | only postcode misses count; email always checked | `test_postcode_guessing_is_braked_but_email_still_works`, `test_wrong_emails_do_not_lock_the_order` |
| BUG-8 | Low | demo page scrolled sideways on phones | scenario chip `shrink-0` | chip wraps (`max-w-[60%]`) | no overflow at 390 px |
| BUG-9 | Low | a test depended on the developer's `.env.local` | `ORDER_SAMPLE_EMAIL_DOMAINS` not pinned | pinned in `conftest.py` | suite green with any `.env.local` |
