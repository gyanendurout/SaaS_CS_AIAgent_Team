# Paddle Damage and Defects — Customer Service Answers

Answers for customers reporting a damaged or faulty paddle. Source: JOOLA warranty
policy (Limited Manufacturers Warranty, sections 2.1–2.3, 2.5, 2.8 and 4.5).
Whether a specific paddle qualifies is decided after JOOLA reviews photos or video
of the damage — never promise a replacement before that review.

## My paddle is delaminating or the face is peeling or bubbling. Is that covered by warranty?

The JOOLA Limited Manufacturers Warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship
under normal use. A paddle face that separates, peels or bubbles can be a
manufacturing defect, so it can be reviewed as a warranty claim. JOOLA decides after
reviewing photo or video documentation of the damage. The claim must be within the
warranty period, from the original purchaser, for a paddle bought from JOOLA or an
authorized retailer, in the United States, Puerto Rico or Canada.

## My paddle core feels crushed, soft or has a dead spot. Can I get a warranty replacement?

A change in how the core plays can be reviewed as a possible manufacturing defect
under the Limited Manufacturers Warranty. JOOLA reviews photo or video documentation
and decides whether it is a defect in materials or workmanship or normal wear and
tear. Normal wear and tear, damage from dropping or mishandling, and accidents are
not covered. The claim must be within the warranty period and from the original
purchaser.

## The edge guard or edge tape on my paddle is coming off. What can I do?

If the edge guard is separating during normal use within the warranty period, it can
be reviewed as a possible workmanship defect — contact JOOLA Customer Service with
photos or video. Damage from hitting the ground, the net post or other impacts counts
as an accident or mishandling and is not covered. Edge tapes sold separately are an
accessory; if one is defective, Customer Service opens a case and asks for photo or
video documentation.

## My paddle cracked or broke. Is it covered?

A crack or break can be reviewed under the Limited Manufacturers Warranty if it comes
from a defect in materials or workmanship under normal use. Damage from dropping,
mishandling, hitting hard objects, accidents, or using the paddle for something it
is not made for is not covered. JOOLA decides after reviewing photo or video
documentation and the proof of purchase.

## What paddle damage is NOT covered by the JOOLA warranty?

The warranty does not cover: normal wear and tear; improper use; negligence or abuse
such as dropping or mishandling; accidents, including not following product
instructions; damage during shipping (except refused deliveries); acts of nature;
fading of graphics (cosmetic); commercial or rental use; repairs or modifications by
anyone other than JOOLA; items bought from unauthorized sellers such as eBay, Facebook
Marketplace or auction sites; and anyone who is not the original purchaser. Even if a
claim doesn't qualify, customers are welcome to contact JOOLA and the team will see
what it can do to help.

## How long is my paddle covered for a defect?

Standard paddles are covered for 6 months from the date of purchase. NFC paddles get
12 months if the paddle is registered in the JOOLA Infinity app within 14 days of
purchase and the receipt is uploaded and approved. If the NFC registration is done
after 14 days, or the receipt isn't approved, the paddle has the standard 6-month
warranty from the purchase date — there are no exceptions to the 14-day window.

## How do I file a warranty claim for a damaged paddle?

Contact JOOLA Customer Service through the case portal on the Contact Us page
(Monday–Friday, 8am–5pm EST, closed on major holidays). Have ready: your full name,
mailing address, daytime phone number, the product SKU and a description of the
problem. JOOLA will then ask for a copy of the original proof of purchase and photo
or video documentation of the damage, and check that the paddle was bought from
JOOLA or an authorized retailer. After review, JOOLA explains the next step: either
shipping the paddle back or a "void and break" process done by the customer following
JOOLA's written instructions.
