# How to Register a JOOLA Paddle

Register a JOOLA paddle through the JOOLA Infinity app for iOS or Android.

In the app, go to More > Product Settings > Product Registration and follow the
instructions.

You can also register without the app, on the JOOLA website, by entering your
information manually and uploading your receipt.

Any JOOLA paddle can be registered, including paddles that are not NFC
chip-enabled and paddles whose owners do not want the extended warranty.

## Registering to extend the warranty

An NFC chip-enabled pickleball paddle bought through the JOOLA website or an
authorised retailer can have its warranty extended from six months to 12 months
by registering it.

To qualify for the extended 12-month coverage:

- Register through the JOOLA Infinity app for iOS or Android
- Register within 14 days of purchase
- Upload a valid digital copy of the purchase receipt during registration
- The receipt must be approved

The extended warranty applies to the US only. It is non-transferable and valid
only for the original purchaser.

## If you cannot find the app

Search for "JOOLA Infinity" in the iOS App Store or on Google Play. JOOLA
customer support can help if you have trouble locating or installing it.
